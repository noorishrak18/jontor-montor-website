My orders

Everything you've ordered from this device, with its current progress. You can cancel an order yourself within 6 hours of placing it.

Ordered from a different phone or browser?

Your orders are saved on the device you ordered from. To check one from anywhere, enter the Order ID from your confirmation along with the phone number you gave us.

What the stages mean

  • New — we've got your order and will confirm it shortly, usually within a few hours.
  • Confirmed — details checked, payment verified if you paid in advance.
  • Printing — your piece is on the machine or being finished and painted.
  • Shipped — handed to the courier or out for delivery.
  • Delivered — done. Thank you.

Something wrong or need to change an address after the cancellation window? Message us — we'd much rather fix it than have you stuck.
