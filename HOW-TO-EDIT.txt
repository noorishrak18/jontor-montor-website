======================================================================
 JONTOR MONTOR — HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
======================================================================

Your whole site is plain text files. You can edit them in any code
editor (Notepad works, but VS Code — free — is nicer), or right inside
GitHub in your browser once it's published (see HOW-TO-PUBLISH.txt).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE THING YOU MUST CHANGE BEFORE GOING LIVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Open:  js/app.js

At the top you'll see a block called CONFIG. Change:

  phone: "8801XXXXXXXXX"
      -> your real WhatsApp number, country code + number, digits only.
         No +, no spaces, no dashes.  e.g.  "8801712345678"

Also set:
  email:     "your@email.com"     <- quote requests by email land here
  facebook:  "https://facebook.com/YOUR-PAGE"
  instagram: "https://instagram.com/YOUR-PAGE"          ("" to hide)
  linkedin:  "https://www.linkedin.com/company/YOUR-COMPANY"  ("" to hide)
  announce:  the yellow scrolling bar text at the very top

Every "Order", "Checkout", "Get a quote" and the floating green button
reads that one phone number. Change it once and the whole site updates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGING PRODUCTS, PROJECTS, AMIGURUMI
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Still in  js/app.js , scroll down to the lists marked "EDIT HERE":

  FAQ                -> what the chat assistant can answer
  DECOR              -> Room decor & statement pieces (Shop page)
  FEATURED_ON_HOME   -> which 4 pieces show on the homepage
                        (use exact names from PRODUCTS or AMI)
  PRODUCTS  -> the Featured Drops cards (Shop page)
  WISHED    -> the "You wished, we delivered" cards (Shop page)
  WORKS     -> the Recent Work cards (homepage)
  AMI       -> the amigurumi cards (Shop page)

Each product line looks like this:

  {name:"Poké Ball (openable)", sub:"Gaming · Pokémon", tag:"Gaming",
   badge:"stock", now:"699", was:"899", img:"images/pokeball.jpg"}

  name  = product name
  sub   = the small grey line
  tag   = little label on the photo
  badge = "stock" (In stock) or "order" (Made to order)
  now   = price (number only) ;  was = old price ("" for none)
  img   = photo file in the images folder

To ADD a product: copy a whole line, paste it above the last one, put a
comma at the end of the line before it, and change the text.
To REMOVE one: delete its whole line (and a leftover comma if needed).
Keep the quotes, colons and commas exactly where they are.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGING HEADLINES / SECTION TEXT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Big titles and paragraphs live in the .html files:
  index.html   = professional homepage (services, capabilities,
                 industries, process, recent work)
  shop.html    = the collectibles shop
  custom.html  = the Get a quote page
Open the page you want and search for
"EDIT HERE". Change the text between  >  and  <  only.
Example:   <h2>Featured drops</h2>   ->   <h2>New this week</h2>

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SWAPPING OR ADDING A PHOTO
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Put your new image in the  images  folder.
2. Keep photos reasonably small (long side ~900px, JPG) so the site
   loads fast. Any free tool like squoosh.app can shrink them.
3. Point a product at it by changing its  img:  line, e.g.
      img:"images/my-new-photo.jpg"

----------------------------------------------------------------------
STATS & REVIEWS (placeholders — put your real numbers)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Stats: in index.html search  data-to  and change the numbers
(240 / 15 / 30 / 7) to your real figures.
Testimonials: in index.html search "TESTIMONIALS" and edit the three
quotes / names / places.
Capabilities & materials tables: in index.html search "MATERIALS TABLE"
— add or remove <tr> rows as you stock new filaments.
Chat assistant answers: js/app.js -> the FAQ list near the top.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
PREVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click  index.html  to open it in your browser. Everything works
locally EXCEPT it needs internet for the fonts. If you edit a file,
just refresh the browser to see the change.

Tip: if a change ever "breaks" the page, you probably deleted a quote,
comma or bracket. Undo (Ctrl+Z) and try again.
======================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING A WHOLE NEW SECTION (e.g. Gaming corner, more decor)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Open  SECTION-TEMPLATES.html  — it's a copy-paste parts box with
ready-made blocks (product row, info cards, dark statement band, new
category tile, FAQ entry) and instructions on each. Every section
takes a background class: band-white, band-tint or band-deep —
alternate them down the page so the scroll stays lively.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SEO / AFTER YOU BUY A DOMAIN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The site ships SEO-ready (titles, descriptions, social-share tags,
structured data for Google, robots.txt and sitemap.xml). They use the
placeholder  https://www.jontormontor.com  for now.
When you buy your real domain, do a find-and-replace (Ctrl+H) of that
placeholder with your domain in: every .html file, robots.txt and
sitemap.xml. That's it.
Then (optional but smart): go to Google Search Console, add your
domain, and submit sitemap.xml — your pages start appearing on Google.
