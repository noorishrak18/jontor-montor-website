About us

The studio that prints parts — and heroes

Jontor Montor (যন্তর মন্তর) is a 3D printing studio in Bangladesh with two lives: a prototyping partner trusted with real engineering work — plus the collectibles shop that made fans fall in love with printing in the first place.

How we got here

We started by making the things no shop in Bangladesh carried — anime katanas, wizard wands, busts and props — printed to order for one customer at a time. Word spread. Then the requests changed: a student needed a hull section for a final defense. A startup needed an enclosure that actually fit their PCB. A firm needed twenty of the same bracket, identical every time.

It turned out the same discipline that makes a collectible worth displaying — clean geometry, tight tolerances, careful finishing — is exactly what prototyping demands. So today, project work is the core of what we do and the shop is where we keep our craft (and our reputation) on public display.

Why the collectibles stay

Some studios would hide the fun side to look "serious." We think it's our best credential. Every katana and bust on the shop page is a public, reviewable sample of our print quality and finishing — made for customers who inspect every millimetre. If we can satisfy a collector, we can satisfy your QA.

The name

যন্তর মন্তর — Jontor Montor — is what you'd call a curious contraption in Bangla, said with a grin. A machine with a bit of magic in it. That's the workshop: real engineering, real craft and the occasional wizard wand.
